Publications

Machine-learning drug discovery, structural bioinformatics and antimicrobial resistance

Publications by Godswill Imolele Anthony: first-author work on machine-learning QSAR and molecular dynamics for tuberculosis and GRK5, plus collaborative work on AMR, immunoinformatics and drug safety.

Ten outputs since 2020 (nine journal articles and one preprint), including two first-author papers on machine-learning-guided drug discovery. My current work extends this toward uncertainty-aware AMR surveillance; see Projects.

Discovery of novel InhA inhibitors by structural bioinformatics and machine-learning QSAR

First and corresponding author

Anthony, G. I., et al. (2026). Discovery of novel InhA inhibitors through structural bioinformatics and machine learning-driven QSAR screening of natural products. Biology Methods and Protocols, 11(1), bpag015. doi:10.1093/biomethods/bpag015

A machine-learning QSAR and structural-bioinformatics workflow for screening natural products against InhA, a validated tuberculosis target, integrating molecular descriptors, model evaluation, docking and molecular dynamics. Figure and details on the Projects page.
